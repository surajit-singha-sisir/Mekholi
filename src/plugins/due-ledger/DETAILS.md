# Due book (বাকির খাতা)

Sell on credit and keep the khata the way the para shop always has — **who owes what, since when** — with the collection buttons where the money actually arrives.

## What it does
- **Due sales** from the till: complete a sale part-paid and the rest goes on the customer's page.
- The **Due book**: every customer with a balance, sorted by who owes most, exportable.
- **Collect** from the till or the customer card; payments settle the **oldest invoices first**.
- Ageing at a glance: how long each due has been standing.

## Good to know
- Free — the khata is not a luxury.
- A customer needs a phone number, because a due without a phone is a donation.
