# Loyalty (lite)

**Points per customer, earned on completed sales.** The smallest loyalty scheme that is still a scheme.

## What it does
- Every completed sale earns the customer points automatically.
- A points balance on the customer, and a screen to see and adjust them.
- Manual corrections for the day the scheme and reality disagree.

## Good to know
- Wants the full machinery — tiers, redemption at the till? That is the **Loyalty** plugin. This one is the notebook version.
