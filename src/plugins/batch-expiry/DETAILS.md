# Batch & Expiry

Track **batch numbers** and **expiry dates**, and hear about stock *before* it dies on the shelf.

## What it does
- Adds batch and expiry fields to every product.
- An **expiry report**: everything expiring inside your horizon (you choose the days), with what is already expired counted separately.
- Expiry shows up where the decisions happen — the product form and the reports.

## How to use it
1. Install, then open any product and fill in batch and expiry.
2. Open **Reports → Expiring stock** to see the horizon.
