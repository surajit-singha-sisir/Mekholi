# Barcode Scanner

Tune how **this device** reads a barcode scanner — and prove it works before the queue does.

## What it does
- Detects scanner input by **typing speed**, so a scan never lands in the wrong box.
- Configurable **prefix/suffix**, speed threshold and an optional **beep** on every read.
- A **live test box**: scan anything and see exactly what the till received.

## Good to know
- Settings are per device — the counter tablet and the office PC can differ.
- Free, and stores nothing on the server.
