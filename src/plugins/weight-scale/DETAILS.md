# Weighing scale

Sell **by weight**: the till reads your scale's barcode labels, and the shop sees which items its scale cannot ring up.

## What it does
- Reads **scale labels** (EAN-13 price/weight embedded): scan, and the line lands at the exact weight — 1.250 kg, not a guess.
- Warns when the label's price disagrees with the shelf price, and charges the shop's own price.
- Shows which weighed products are missing the codes the scale needs.
