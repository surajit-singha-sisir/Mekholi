# Loyalty

**Points, tiers and redemption**: what the shop owes its best customers, and money off the sale when they spend it.

## What it does
- Points earned per taka on completed sales, at rates you set.
- **Tiers** — regulars climb, and each tier can earn faster.
- **Redemption at the till**: points become money off, right on the payment screen.
- Every movement is on the customer's card: earned, spent, adjusted, and by whom.
