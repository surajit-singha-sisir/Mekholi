# Notifications

A **bell on the top bar that watches the shop** — so the shopkeeper does not have to.

## What it does
- **Stock running out**: products at or under their reorder point (and the ones already at zero).
- **Stock transfers**: what moved between branches in the last day — how many units, from where, to where — so a branch hears what arrived and what left.
- **Warranty expiry**: promises about to lapse within 30 days, and ones that already have, so no claim is honoured by mistake.
- **Dues past your threshold**: the khata's slow payers, surfaced.
- **How today is going**: takings against a normal day.
- You choose which of them may ring, and how often, in the plugin settings.
