# Notifications

A **bell on the top bar that watches the shop** — so the shopkeeper does not have to.

## What it does
- **Stock running out**: products at or under their reorder point.
- **Dues past your threshold**: the khata's slow payers, surfaced.
- **How today is going**: takings against a normal day.
- You choose which of them may ring, and how often, in the plugin settings.
