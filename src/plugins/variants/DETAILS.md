# Variants

Build a product's variants from its **options** — size, colour, capacity — and price them in one pass.

## What it does
- Define options once (Size: S/M/L · Colour: red/blue) and the **combinations build themselves**.
- Price every variant in one grid — or let them inherit the product's price.
- Each variant carries its own SKU and stock; the till sells the exact one.
