# Printer Setup

Pair a **thermal printer** over Bluetooth, USB or the network — and design what the printed invoice says.

## What it does
- Guided pairing for the printers BD shops actually buy (58mm and 80mm thermal).
- A **receipt designer**: header, footer, what shows and what does not.
- A test print button, because the first print should not be a customer's.

## Good to know
- Always on and free — it configures this device and stores nothing on the server.
