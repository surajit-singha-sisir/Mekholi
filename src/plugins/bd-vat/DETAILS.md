# Bangladesh VAT (Mushak)

The **NBR Mushak-6.3 tax invoice**, done properly: your BIN on the paper, the VAT line on every sale, and a plain-words guide to the মূসক system.

## What it does
- Prints a **Mushak-6.3** compliant tax invoice with the shop's BIN.
- Carries the VAT breakdown on every sale, at the rates you configure.
- Includes a **plain-language guide** to the VAT system — what to file, when, and which paper is which.

## How to use it
1. Install, then enter the shop's **BIN** in the plugin settings.
2. Sell as usual — the invoice grows its VAT identity automatically.
