# Branches

Open branches and **watch them from the head office**: today and this week per branch, dues out, and the branch switcher on every till.

## What it does
- **Open a branch** — each with its own stock rooms, registers and staff access.
- The **branch switcher** appears in the top bar the moment a second branch exists.
- A **Branches screen**: today's and this week's takings per branch, side by side.
- A **Sales by branch** report and a dashboard widget for the owner's morning glance.
- Staff can be limited to their own branch from the Staff screen.

## Good to know
- Until this plugin is installed, the whole app behaves as a clean one-branch shop — no branch questions anywhere.
- Uninstalling keeps every branch's data; the screens simply step back.
