# Serial Numbers

Track **every unit by its own number** — IMEI, engine number, case number — from delivery to invoice.

## What it does
- Receive stock with serials; each unit becomes its own traceable thing.
- Sell by serial: the invoice says exactly **which unit** left the shop.
- Look up any serial later — when it arrived, when it sold, to whom.
- The natural partner of **Warranty**: a promise attached to a specific unit.
