# Multi-Currency Display

Read every amount in a **second currency** — without ever confusing the books.

## What it does
- Shows a second currency beside amounts across the shop: pick USD, EUR, INR, SAR, AED… any of them.
- **Live exchange rate** when online, the cached one when not, and **your own rate** when you know better.
- The books never move: every sale, report and export stays in the shop's base currency. The second currency is a reading aid, not an accounting change.
