# Label printing

Print **barcode labels from your own SKUs** — for the half of the shelf no factory ever labelled.

## What it does
- Pick products, set copies, print — on **A4 sticker sheets** or **thermal label rolls**.
- Eight built-in sizes plus **custom sizes** in millimetres, straight off the packet you bought.
- A **partly used A4 sheet** is fine: tell it how many stickers are gone and the run starts where they are.
- Each label can carry the shop name, product name, price (with **MRP**), the SKU digits, a packed date and one extra line — your phone number, say.
- A **live, actual-size preview** before a single sticker is spent.
